Why work with me

Eric Price founded Boulder Tech Companion after six years as a Systems Engineer at Salesforce, where he earned three Salesforce certifications building and troubleshooting business-critical systems. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and graduated from Codecraft coding school in 2017 — pairing formal theory with hands-on, production-ready coding skills.

Eric is currently pursuing his CompTIA A+ certification to deepen his hardware and device-level troubleshooting expertise, and he regularly consults with friends working in cybersecurity to stay current on the latest scam tactics and defense strategies.

That combination — enterprise systems experience, hands-on technical training, and a direct line to cybersecurity expertise — is what makes Boulder Tech Companion different: not just tech help, but tech help grounded in the same rigor used to protect large-scale business systems, now applied to protecting individuals and families from scams.
